Master Japanese Scripts

Japanese has two syllabic writing systems known as kana — Hiragana and Katakana. Explore both with interactive charts, audio pronunciation, and quizzes.

The foundational Japanese script used for native words, grammar, and pronunciation guides.

Used for foreign loanwords, onomatopoeia, scientific terms, and emphasis in Japanese writing.
